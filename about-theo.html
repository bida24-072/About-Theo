About

A short introduction to who I am, what I do, and why I do it.

A student. A builder.
A Motswana.

I'm a Business Analysis & Computing student at BAC with a deep obsession for building things that live on the internet. While most of my classmates focus on theory, I spend my evenings shipping real websites for real businesses — boutiques, restaurants, lodges, salons, vape stores.

Every project teaches me something new about design, code, and what Botswana actually needs online. From e-commerce cart systems to WhatsApp order flows to full data dashboards — I build tools that solve real problems for real people.

My goal is simple: prove that world-class digital work can come out of Gaborone. Not someday. Right now.

Vision

I don't just build websites.
I build evidence that Botswana
can compete globally.

The digital tools that power businesses around the world — e-commerce, booking systems, data dashboards, analytics — shouldn't be a privilege reserved for companies in Johannesburg, Nairobi, or London. They should be standard here, in Gaborone, in Francistown, in Maun.

Every project I ship is a small push toward that reality. A boutique with a working cart. A restaurant with a real online presence. A lodge with a booking system. Each one proves that we don't need to wait for someone else to build this for us.
